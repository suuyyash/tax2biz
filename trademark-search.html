Nice Classification

Goods (Classes 1–34) vs Services (Classes 35–45)

Every product or commercial service belongs to one of 45 globally recognized Nice classes. Choosing the right class ensures airtight legal coverage.

Classes 1 to 34

Physical Goods & Products

Protects physical manufactured commodities, packaged goods, hardware, apparel, and chemicals:

  • Class 9: Software, mobile apps, electronics, gadgets, AI hardware
  • Class 25: Clothing, readymade garments, footwear, headwear
  • Class 5: Pharmaceuticals, ayurvedic remedies, health supplements
  • Class 30: Coffee, tea, snacks, spices, confectionery, bakery
  • Class 3: Cosmetics, perfumes, skincare, beauty and grooming soaps
Classes 35 to 45

Digital & Commercial Services

Protects business operations, digital platforms, service delivery, and consultancy activities:

  • Class 42: SaaS, cloud hosting, software & website development
  • Class 35: E-commerce retail portals, advertising, business consulting
  • Class 36: Fintech, online banking, loans, investments, insurance
  • Class 43: Restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, meal delivery services
  • Class 41: EdTech platforms, training, coaching, media production

What Indian Trademark Application Statuses Mean

Registered

Registration certificate issued. Full nationwide legal monopoly active for 10 years under Section 28.

Objected (Sec 9 / 11)

Registry issued an examination report. A formal written response by a trademark attorney is required within 30 days.

Advertised in Journal

Accepted and published in the Trade Marks Journal for a 4-month statutory public opposition period before certificate issue.

Abandoned / Refused

Application lapsed or rejected due to missed deadlines, unfiled examination replies, or failed opposition hearing.

Registry Glossary

Understanding IP India Application Statuses

What each status reported by the Trade Marks Registry (CGPDTM) means for your brand.

Marked for Exam

Marked for Exam

Your application is assigned to a trademark examiner to review distinctiveness and check Section 9 & 11 conflicts.

Objected

Objected (Sec 9 / 11)

An examination report has been issued. A formal legal reply drafted by a trademark attorney must be filed within 30 days.

Advertised / Accepted

Accepted & Advertised

The examiner accepted your trademark. It is published in the official Trademark Journal for a 4-month public opposition window.

Registered

Registered (® Certificate)

No oppositions were filed. The Registrar has issued your official Certificate of Registration, valid for 10 years across India.
