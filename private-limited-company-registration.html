Overview of Private Limited Company Structure

A Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) is India’s most trusted business entity under the Companies Act, 2013. It establishes an independent legal identity offering limited liability protection, seamless equity division, and high credibility among investors and banks.

Feature Private Limited (Pvt Ltd) One Person Company (OPC) Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Sole Proprietorship
Legal Identity Separate Legal Entity Separate Legal Entity Separate Legal Entity No Separate Identity
Liability of Owners Limited to Unpaid Shares Limited to Unpaid Shares Limited to Contribution Unlimited Personal Liability
Members / Partners Min 2, Max 200 1 Member (+ 1 Nominee) Min 2, No Max Limit Only 1 Person
Minimum Capital ₹0 (No Minimum) ₹0 (No Minimum) ₹0 (No Minimum) No Requirement
Investor & VC Preference High (Issues Equity Shares) Low (Only 1 Shareholder) Medium (Cannot Issue Shares) Not Eligible for Equity
Foreign Direct Investment 100% Automatic Route Not Permitted Selected Sectors Not Permitted

Key Characteristics and Benefits

Choosing a Private Limited structure establishes an institutional foundation built for long-term scalability and financial security.

Limited Liability Protection

Personal assets of directors and shareholders remain completely protected from company debts, legal claims, and corporate losses.

Separate Legal Entity

The company can hold properties, enter commercial contracts, open dedicated bank accounts, and sue or be sued in its own distinct corporate name.

Perpetual Succession

The existence of the company is uninterrupted by changes in shareholding or the retirement, bankruptcy, or death of any director or member.

Equity Fundraising Readiness

Venture capital funds, angel investors, and accelerators require a private limited structure to issue preferred shares, equity, or convertible debentures.

High Institutional Credibility

Public registry status with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs enhances trust with banks, enterprise vendors, corporate customers, and government tenders.

100% Foreign Direct Investment

Foreign nationals and foreign corporate entities can invest up to 100% equity in most sectors under the automatic approval route.

Eligibility Requirements for Incorporation

Statutory parameters prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013 for online company registration:

Minimum Two Directors (Natural Persons)

At least two natural persons above 18 years of age. At least one director must be an Indian Resident who stayed in India for ≥ 182 days in the previous financial year.

Minimum Two Shareholders

Can be the same individuals as the directors, or separate individuals and corporate entities. The maximum shareholder limit is 200 members.

Zero Minimum Capital Requirement

No statutory minimum paid-up capital threshold. You can incorporate with any authorized capital (commonly ₹10,000 or ₹1,00,000).

Physical Registered Office Address in India

A valid Indian address within the jurisdiction of the relevant ROC. Can be owned, rented, or residential premises accompanied by an owner NOC.

Unique Corporate Name (Rule 8 Compliance)

The proposed name must be unique, descriptive of your activities, and must not conflict with existing registered companies or protected trademarks.

Class-3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC)

All proposed directors must possess Class-3 Digital Signatures to electronically sign the SPICe+ statutory forms on the MCA portal.

MCA Company Name Approval Guidelines (Rule 8 Checklist)

Company name rejection is the #1 cause of incorporation delays in India. Under the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014, your proposed name must strictly adhere to three distinct components:

Approved Formula

[Unique Distinct Prefix] + [Activity / Object Word] + [Private Limited]

1. Unique Brand Prefix

Must be completely distinctive and must not closely resemble or phonetically mimic any existing company or LLP name registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

2. Descriptive Business Object

Must clearly reflect the primary commercial activity stated in your main MOA object clause (e.g. "Technologies", "Logistics", "Enterprises", "Consulting", "Retail").

3. No Trademark Conflicts (Class Search)

The proposed name must not infringe on any registered trademark or pending trademark application under the Trademarks Act, 1999 across relevant classes.

4. Restricted Words & Approvals

Words like "National", "Bharat", "Federal", "Bank", "Insurance", or "Stock Exchange" require prior statutory regulatory permissions before filing.

Approved Format Examples
  • ✓ Nexura Cloud Technologies Private Limited
  • ✓ Ardent Agro Logistics Private Limited
  • ✓ BluePeak FinServ Advisors Private Limited
  • ✓ Vigora Green Energy Private Limited
Rejected Patterns (Rule 8 Breaches)
  • ✕ Tata Cloud Solutions (Infringes well-known trademark)
  • ✕ Best Tech Services (Too generic / no unique prefix)
  • ✕ Infosys Consulting Group (Identical/deceptive similarity)
  • ✕ National Digital Bank (Restricted term without RBI approval)

Post-Registration Mandatory Compliances

Statutory responsibilities required to keep your new company compliant under MCA regulations:

Within 180 Days

Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business)

Directors must deposit agreed share capital into the corporate bank account and file a verified declaration with the MCA before initiating business operations.

Within 30 Days

Form ADT-1 (Auditor Appointment)

The Board of Directors must appoint the company's first Statutory Chartered Accountant auditor within 30 days of incorporation.

Within 60 Days

Issuance of Share Certificates

Physical or electronic stamped share certificates must be issued to all initial subscribers against their paid-up capital contribution.

Annual ROC Filings

Form AOC-4 & Form MGT-7

Mandatory annual submission of audited financial statements (AOC-4) and annual returns (MGT-7) with the Registrar of Companies every fiscal year.
